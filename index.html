Player Profile

Game Guide

Exploration

Explore
Your default way to traverse the realms is by exploring. Select a zone and click "Explore" to search it, uncovering wild väsen to tame, items for taming, sacred wells that heal your väsen, or hidden runes.

Note: All items dropped in a zone belong to its naturally occuring väsen.

Taming

Tame
Tame a väsen by offering it its desired item, and then defeating it. Find out the desired item by carefully reading the description or interrogating the väsen during combat. Offering is a free action. You can offer up to items per combat, but once the väsen has its desired item it won't accept any more.

Health

Health (HP)
At 0 the väsen is knocked out.

Post-combat heal
Health persists between combat encounters. Active party heals health (even from 0 health) after combat.

Sacred well heal
The sacred well is encountered by exploring. Once encountered, all väsen (including those not in party) heal health.

Item heal
Items can be used to heal or health to the väsen depending on if it's the correct taming item.

Auto Heal
Use this button to use items to automatically heal active party, although it's not recommended for managing scarce items.

Note: All healing is based on max health, not current.

Megin

Megin (MP)
Used to perform skills.

Post-combat megin refill
Megin fully refills after each combat.

Combat megin refill
megin refilled per turn.

Same-element Discount
Skills aligned with the väsen's own element cost less megin.

Note: All megin refill is based on max megin, not current.

Attributes

Strength (STR)
Increases damage with strength attacks.

Wisdom (WIS)
Increases damage with wisdom attacks.

Defense (DEF)
Reduces damage taken from strength attacks.

Durability (DUR)
Reduces damage taken from wisdom attacks.

Attribute Stages

Attribute Stages
Some skills, runes and traits raise or lower attribute stages. Each stage increases or decreases the affected attribute by . Stages range from to .

Debuffed
Väsen with one or more negative attribute stages are refered to as "debuffed".

Skills

Use Skills
Skills are the moves your väsen use in combat. Attack skills deal damage, while utility skills have other effects. Each skill is marked with its type:
Strength Attack: scales with strength.
Wisdom Attack: scales with wisdom.
Mixed Attack: scales with both strength and wisdom.
Utility: buffs, debuffs and other effects.

Power
Power is a value shown on each skill that represents the basic damage multiplier, which is scaled by the associated damage attribute (strength/wisdom).

Initial Bonus
Certain utility skills have an "initial bonus" that grants extra attribute stages on their first use per combat.

Empower
Some attack skills empower the user, increasing the damage of its next attack by 12%.

Skill Priority
Swapping > Utility Skills > Attack Skills.
The player moves first inside each category.

Runes

Equip Runes
Equip runes to your Väsen for powerful effects. At level , they gain the skill to equip two runes.

Auto Equip Runes
Use this button to instantly equip a random selection of viable runes for the active party.

Bind Runes
Certain pairs of runes, when equipped together, awaken a hidden combined effect of their own. Experiment with different combinations to discover them.

Challenges

Guardian Battles
Every zone except Ginnungagap is watched over by a Guardian fielding a team of 3 Väsen. Click "Challenge Guardian" to face them. Defeating a Guardian for the first time fully restores your party's health and Megin, permanently unlocks the next zone, and grants rewards.

Endless Tower
Once Ginnungagap is unlocked, you may challenge the Endless Tower: an ever-climbing gauntlet of floors that grow tougher the deeper you go. Väsen cannot be tamed within, and your team is periodically restored and renewed as you climb. Your highest floor and the team you used are saved as a permanent record.

Permadeath-free zone
Idunn's apples keep every väsen, and its runes, safe within the tower. Even with the Permadeath or Lost Runes rules of Ragnarök Mode, väsen that fall in the tower are restored when the run ends.

Max Level

Level Milestones

Combat Actions

Swap
Costs a turn. The incoming väsen is "Preparing" and can't use skills until next turn.

Offer Item
Free action. Limited per combat, wild väsen only. Once the desired item is given, no more can be offered.

Interrogate
Costs a turn. Ask a wild väsen what it desires.

Pass
Skip your turn.

Flee
Retreat and end the combat. The väsen on the field stays behind to cover the retreat and is knocked out, and your party is left at very low health. In Ragnarök Mode that knockout follows the rules (Permadeath, Lost Runes), and closing or reloading the page during a battle counts as fleeing.

Auto Battle
Let your väsen fight on their own. Press again to take back control.
